# PRD: Open House Platform Expansion

## 1. Product Name
VertexAgent Open House Platform Expansion

## 2. Product Summary
VertexAgent already supports live listing retrieval, dual QR flows, client-side validation, Twilio phone verification, Firestore lead storage, consent capture, agent email alerts, and webhook-based CRM syncing. This expansion turns the current open-house experience into a more complete platform layer.

The expansion adds offline-capable sign-in, richer branded sign-in templates, listing-level question configuration, dynamic QR management, automated follow-up, listing microsites, improved analytics, and eventually team, broker, and lender operations.

## 3. Problem Statement
The current experience is strong in lead validation and routing, but it is still lighter in guest experience packaging, listing presentation, offline resilience, configurable open-house flows, and organizational controls. Agents and brokers need an open-house system that does more than capture a lead record. They need a reliable event workflow, branded property presentation, better post-event follow-up, and configuration controls that scale across listings, agents, teams, and brokerages.

## 4. Goals
- Make the open-house flow reliable in real-world event conditions, including low-connectivity scenarios.
- Improve the guest-facing experience through branded sign-in layouts, cleaner listing presentation, and mobile-first routes.
- Turn QR codes into persistent marketing infrastructure, not just one-off links.
- Add listing-level configuration for questions, follow-up, disclosures, and microsites.
- Strengthen the agent workflow with analytics, notes, follow-up, and clear CRM delivery.
- Prepare the platform for team and brokerage operations.

## 5. MVP Scope
### Include in MVP
- Offline sign-in and sync.
- Branded sign-in layouts.
- Listing-level custom questions.
- Listing microsites.
- Dynamic QR destination management.
- Automated follow-up texts.
- Lead workspace notes and verification visibility.
- Basic analytics dashboard.
